Authorized Research & Sandbox Terms of Service

Governing academic experimentation, ethical red-teaming, and sandbox containment standards for the ChameleonShell platform.

1. Authorized Enterprise Sandbox Environment

The ChameleonShell deception engine and web dashboard are provisioned strictly for defensive cybersecurity research, active deception evaluation, and security engineering testing within controlled sandboxes.

2. Prohibited Offensive Actions

Users, red-team evaluators, and researchers agree not to utilize the honeypot infrastructure as an offensive staging jump host, to route attacks toward third-party systems, or to circumvent Docker container sandbox boundaries.

3. Containment & Denial of Service Safeguards

The engine incorporates automated rate-limiting, AST sanitization, and network namespace isolation to guarantee that simulated exploits cannot compromise the underlying host operating system.

4. Proprietary Intellectual Property & Architecture Rights

The ChameleonShell architecture, Virtual State Tree formulation, and autonomous rule generation engine are the intellectual property of ChameleonShell Security Systems.
