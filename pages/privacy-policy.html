Threat Telemetry & Data Privacy Policy

Governing honeypot sensor telemetry capture, IP address anonymization, GDPR compliance, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act standards.

1. Boundaries of Sensor Telemetry Collection

ChameleonShell operates as an isolated research deception platform. All inbound network traffic captured on containerized SSH (port 2222), HTTP (port 80), and IMDSv2 metadata endpoints represents unsolicited probing by automated bots, red-team scanners, or authenticated researchers within an authorized sandbox environment.

2. Anonymization & Cryptographic Hashing

Attacker IP addresses captured during wild deployments are salted and cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 before ingestion into public telemetry streams or conference paper benchmark evaluations. No personally identifiable information (PII) of legitimate users is captured or stored.

3. Canary Token Safety & Harmless Decoys

All credentials, AWS IAM secrets, and database strings hosted in the Virtual State Tree (e.g. /var/www/html/.env, .aws/credentials) are synthetic decoy honeytokens. They possess zero access permissions to external infrastructure and serve purely as detection tripwires.

4. Enterprise Research & Data Governance

This platform is maintained by the ChameleonShell Threat Research Group. All threat telemetry and adversarial sequence captures adhere strictly to responsible disclosure standards and active defense best practices.
